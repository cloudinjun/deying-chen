The design question

Independent modules, many arrangements.

I designed the desk around adjustable divisions and interchangeable uses. Each module has its own frame and four casters. Cabinets, counters, shelves and signage move together, with a small joint between neighboring units.

The browser prototype develops an earlier native Grasshopper study into independently movable units. Dimensions follow the displayed arrangement; “Spread” measures the separated view. The downloadable Grasshopper file documents the earlier shared-frame study. Implementation and verification were AI-assisted.

A digital design study. Connections, locking hardware, loads and physical assembly still need development.
